# Third-Party Integrations

This is a collection of community-contributed guides for integrating
third-party packages with Yii3.

- The Yii community creates the guides.
- Yii 核心团队成员负责策划和编辑。

欢迎提交你自己的文章。团队成员将审查它，提供反馈并以最佳方式合并。
